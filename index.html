FAQ

The honest answers.

Will it actually block my apps? +
Yes. MonkeyLock uses Apple's Screen Time API — the same enforcement Apple uses for parental controls. Selected apps are locked when your daily balance hits zero and unlocked as you earn more. There's no in-app override or workaround.
How much screen time do I start with each day? +
In default mode, each day starts with a portion of your chosen daily goal — personalized from your onboarding answers, then you earn the rest through exercise, brain games, steps, or photos. In No Bananas mode (opt-in for the hardcore), you start at 0 and earn every minute, but at 1.5× the normal rate.
What happens when my balance hits zero? +
Selected apps shield immediately. Tapping a blocked app shows the MonkeyLock shield with a button to earn more. No grace period, no "just one more minute." Earn through exercise, a brain game, steps, or an outdoor photo to unlock.
Does the camera record me? +
No. Sweat Drip Verification runs entirely on-device. Frames are processed and discarded as they come in — nothing is recorded, nothing is uploaded, nothing trains a model.
What if I'm injured or can't exercise? +
Brain games, Apple Health steps, and outdoor photos all cover non-exercise paths. Brain games alone can earn up to 90 minutes a day by default (you can tune the cap in Settings), and steps + photos add more on top — no exercise required.
Why no monthly plan or lifetime option? +
Behavior change happens over months. Annual fits the product. We'd rather ship one honest plan than three competing tiers.
How does the 7-day free trial work? Can I cancel? +
Yes. Sign up, get full access for 7 days, cancel any time before the trial ends from Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions and you won't be charged. After the trial, your card is charged the annual rate; you can cancel future renewals at any time but past charges aren't refundable through us — Apple handles refund requests directly.
Will my data sync across devices? +
Yes. Profile data syncs to iCloud — workouts, brain games, habits, streaks, and settings. New device, same Apple ID, your progress restores. Never to our servers.
Can a kid or teen use this? +
MonkeyLock is rated 4+ but designed for self-directed adults working on their own habits. The Screen Time API requires the device's primary Apple ID; if you're managing a child's device through Family Sharing, Apple's built-in Screen Time controls are the better tool.
Can I just delete the app to bypass the block? +
You could. But if you're reading this question, you already know that defeats the point. The lock only works if you commit to it. We trust you to be honest with yourself — that's the whole product.
